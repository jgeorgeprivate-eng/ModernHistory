# Modern History

A study app for VCE Modern History Units 1 and 2. Lessons, recall quizzes and written practice earn coins, and the coins build a 1920s party.

It has 71 lessons in seven blocks, 339 quiz questions, 71 written practice questions with model answers and marking checklists, a quick-fire round for each block, an evidence bank of 210 facts and a party of 16 items and 12 guests.

- Unit 1, Change and conflict: the peace settlement after World War One, the Weimar Republic and Hitler's rise to power, social change for Jewish people in Nazi Germany, and the causes of World War Two.
- Unit 2, The changing world order: the causes of the Cold War, Cold War conflicts and tensions, and challenge and change.

It is a static site with no build step. Open `index.html`, or serve the folder with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root.

Progress is saved in the browser on the device being used.
